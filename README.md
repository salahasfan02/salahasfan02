### 🧠 What I care about

Most "AI apps" quietly ship your data to someone else's server. I build the opposite kind — tools where the model runs on your hardware via **[Ollama](https://ollama.com)**, your data stays in a folder you control, and every AI claim can be traced back to something real rather than invented.
